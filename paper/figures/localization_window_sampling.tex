\caption{Tool-vs-tool divergence localization on \texttt{div\_window\_sampling}
  (\textsc{Breach} vs.\ \textsc{TaLiRo}; no \textsc{native} baseline). On a
  non-uniform grid with a wide segment $[0,3]$ the buried $G_{[1,2]}$ reduces
  over a window lying entirely between samples. \textsc{Breach} uses the
  interpolated predicate value at the window start; \textsc{TaLiRo} finds no
  sample inside the closed window and returns the always-identity. The two
  agree everywhere else (shaded nodes are the propagation path), so the
  localizer reports the single origin $G_{[1,2]}$
  ($\mathtt{WindowMin}$ vs.\ $\mathtt{SampleMin}$). Both \emph{satisfy} the
  spec; the divergence is a value-only gap ($+2.0$ vs.\ $+5.0$), with no
  syntactic giveaway in the formula.}
